# USER PERSONAS

**Project:** ArenaMind

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** User Personas

---

# Purpose

ArenaMind serves multiple user groups, each with different goals, permissions, challenges, and interaction patterns.

This document defines the primary personas that guide product decisions, UI/UX design, AI behavior, and feature development.

Every feature implemented in ArenaMind must solve a real problem for at least one persona.

---

# Persona 1 — Fan

## Overview

The Fan is the primary user of ArenaMind.

Their goal is to enjoy the event with minimal stress while receiving personalized, real-time assistance.

---

### Profile

Role: Stadium Visitor

Age Range: 16–70

Technical Skill: Beginner to Advanced

Primary Device: Smartphone

Session Duration: 3–8 Hours

---

### Goals

- Find the correct entrance
- Navigate inside the stadium
- Reach assigned seats
- Locate food and restrooms
- Avoid crowded areas
- Receive live match updates
- Access emergency help quickly

---

### Pain Points

- Getting lost
- Long queues
- Traffic confusion
- Language barriers
- Lack of live information

---

### AI Assistance

Fan AI provides:

- Smart navigation
- Voice assistant
- Route optimization
- Personalized recommendations
- Multilingual communication
- Emergency guidance

---

### Permissions

✔ View navigation

✔ AI Chat

✔ Notifications

✔ Food Discovery

✔ Accessibility Tools

✔ Transport Planner

✘ Administrative Controls

---

### Success Criteria

The fan completes the entire event journey without confusion or unnecessary delays.

# Design Principles

Every interface should adapt based on the active persona.

ArenaMind should never expose unnecessary information or controls.

Users should only see features relevant to their responsibilities.

This reduces cognitive load, improves usability, and enhances security.

---

# Final Statement

ArenaMind is designed around people—not screens.

Every persona represents a real stakeholder whose experience should be improved through intelligent, accessible, and context-aware AI assistance.
