# Overview

ArenaMind has been designed by first analyzing every requirement in the official problem statement and then mapping each requirement to one or more dedicated product features.

This document ensures that every implemented feature has a measurable purpose and directly contributes toward solving a real operational problem inside a smart stadium.

# Official Problem Statement

Build a Generative AI enabled solution that enhances stadium operations and the overall tournament experience for fans, organizers, volunteers, and venue staff.

The solution should leverage Generative AI to improve:

- Navigation
- Crowd Management
- Accessibility
- Transportation
- Sustainability
- Multilingual Assistance
- Operational Intelligence
- Real-Time Decision Support

# Requirement 1 — Navigation

## Challenge

Visitors often struggle to locate entrances, parking zones, seats, food courts, washrooms, exits, and emergency facilities.

## ArenaMind Solution

ArenaMind provides an AI-powered navigation engine capable of understanding natural language requests.

Examples:

- "Take me to Gate 4."
- "Find the nearest accessible washroom."
- "Guide me to my seat."
- "Where is the closest medical station?"

Navigation is updated dynamically based on crowd density, restricted zones, and live incidents.

### AI Modules

- Navigation AI
- Route Optimizer
- Indoor Positioning Engine

Expected Impact: **High**
